This piece outlines three validation techniques construction estimators can use to catch scope omissions: checking quantity ratios against project type standards, accounting for MEP systems in high-density areas, and identifying exception clauses in drawing notes that alter scope requirements. Guest expert Sampada Chavan contributes practical patterns drawn from real estimating workflows. The result is a checklist approach to preventing costly scope gaps before they reach bid submission.
