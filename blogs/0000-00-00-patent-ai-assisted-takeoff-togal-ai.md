Togal.AI has secured five U.S. patents with six additional pending, protecting its AI systems for construction takeoff and plan interpretation. The company trained its platform on a proprietary dataset of more than 100,000 construction drawings curated by architects and engineers since being founded in 2019. The patents signal Togal.AI's leadership in establishing defensible, enterprise-ready AI infrastructure for preconstruction workflows.
