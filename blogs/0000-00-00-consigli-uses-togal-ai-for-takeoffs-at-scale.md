Consigli Construction, a major general contractor with 80-100 estimators across 15+ offices, transitioned from legacy On-Screen Takeoff software to Togal.AI's cloud-based platform to improve collaboration and efficiency. The cloud-based solution eliminated performance issues with remote teams, enabled real-time parallel work across estimators, and freed staff to focus on strategic analysis. This shift enhanced estimate quality, coordination, and credibility in client presentations.
