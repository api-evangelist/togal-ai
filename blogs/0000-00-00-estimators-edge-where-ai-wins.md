This piece examines practical applications of AI in construction estimating, arguing that AI delivers the most value by automating repetitive tasks like tracing and measuring rather than replacing estimators entirely. Successful firms treat AI as a productivity tool that frees skilled professionals to focus on higher-value decision-making and complex scope interpretation. The article acknowledges that construction's inherent variability makes fully autonomous estimating unrealistic.
