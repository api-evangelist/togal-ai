This article explores how AI-assisted takeoff software should improve estimators' work experience rather than just increase speed. The authors present a framework with five evaluation questions to help construction professionals assess whether technology genuinely reduces burnout or simply adds more pressure while automating repetitive tasks. Real value comes from tools that allow estimators to focus on judgment-intensive work rather than manual clicking and tracing.
